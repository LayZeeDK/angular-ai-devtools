# M049 interaction-story — synthesis

Four tasks in four waves, reviewed and landed. This records what the milestone measured, the two
decisions that shaped it, and what it cost.

## What the milestone set out to show

The roadmap said the panel narrates the interaction sequence a page recorded, each step naming what
the user did and what the app emitted. T001 measured both halves. The second exists: three streams in
arrival order — `routerEvents` (`agent-main.js:1474`), `pendingWorkEvents` (`:639`) and
`boundaryEntries` (`:1942`). The first does not exist at all: `interaction` occurs zero times in
`extension/agent-main.js` and zero times across the twenty-eight `extension/*.js` files, and no file
registers a `click`, `pointer*`, `mouse*` or `key*` listener. The only document-level listeners are
`error` (`:2550`) and `unhandledrejection` (`:2557`), and both record failures.

So the milestone narrates what the app emitted, and says nothing about what a user did — because
nothing records it.

## The question that decided the shape, and its answer

Whether the three streams can be merged honestly was unmeasured, and it decided whether the milestone
ships one story or three sequences. T001 answered it from the code rather than from observation: one
reader, `nowMs()` at `extension/agent-main.js:728`, sits behind all three assignment lines (`:1638`,
`:699`, `:2073`), and the comment above it states they are "the same measurement on the same scale".
So a merge sorted by `at` is a measurement.

It also measured what is absent: `seq`, `sequence` and `arrivalIndex` occur zero times. Two events
sharing an instant therefore have no recorded order, and the narration renders them as one instant
rather than inventing a tie-break from stream name, type or alphabetical position. Two mutants exist
solely to keep that true — one adds a secondary sort on stream name, one on `fields.type`, and both
redden with one sentence naming the defect.

## The second decision: a story is what the record kept

All three streams are bounded and drop their oldest entries — 512, 64 and 128. The narration therefore
carries `BOUNDED_STATEMENT` into every render. A surface that showed a merged sequence without it
would let a bounded buffer read as a complete history, which is the same defect class as reporting an
absent observable as clean.

## The one widening, stated rather than hidden

A panel surface has no runtime imports, so the pane cannot import the join's bounded statement. The
demo hands it over as a fifth `bounded` field beside the four contract fields. That is a deliberate
widening and it is recorded in the module, the task Log and the route record, rather than left for a
reader to discover a fifth key and wonder whether the contract was broken.

## What it cost, and the pattern

The product work landed cleanly. What cost landings was prose and pins that restate a measured number:
M049 moved the copy record from 20 surfaces to 21 and from 122 sentences to 135, against 133 distinct
digest rows because two sentences are declared byte-for-byte by more than one surface. Six sites had
to follow — the demo fact, its comment, the join's inline-label count, the copy pane's own paragraph,
and two quotes inside the route record. The copy suite caught each one by quoting the line it found
rather than trusting a number, which is why none of them shipped.

For the third milestone running, `research/raw/m049-verdict-claims.json` did not exist and eight
navigation-blocking tests plus two overlay tests read it. It is a derived recording with its own
capture, and no task declares it. That is now a pattern rather than an incident: the file should be
declared by the task that runs the project verify, or generated by `gsd land` itself.

## What ships

The `interaction-story` surface rendering `steps` and `cannot-tell`; a pure narration over three
bounded streams with a closed four-word reason set; seven mutants; and a story that states its own
bound in every render.
