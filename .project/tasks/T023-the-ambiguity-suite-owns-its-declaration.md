# T023 — The ambiguity suite owns its declaration

## Context

T021 made the ambiguity suite pin the harness match set by **reading
`.project/tasks/T002-harness-target-page.md:48-54`**. The final panel proved what that costs: the ship flow
archives `.project/tasks/`, so the moment M023 ships the suite fails with `ENOENT` and stays red.

A held-out test may not read the milestone's own bookkeeping. `.project/` is not stable across the milestone's
lifecycle — `tasks/` is archived by ship, `review/` is regenerated by every cycle, and both move under
`.project/archive/`. A fixture contract has to live with the fixture's tests.

## Approach

1. Carry the declaration in the suite: the match set, the `id` exclusion and the reasoning become a constant
   the suite owns, with the origin recorded as provenance (`T002`, `<commit>`, the sentences it quoted) rather
   than as a path it reads.
2. Keep every assertion T021 added: the distinguishing binding reddens, the harmless attribute stays green,
   `!isMatchedName('id')`, and the reader sees `id` on both hosts with differing values.
3. Prove the fix against the change that broke it: with `.project/tasks/` absent, the suite must still pass.
